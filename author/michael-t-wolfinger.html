What We Do

Expert consulting across the RNA design pipeline

From raw sequence to functional RNA molecule — computational, structural, and experimental design support for academic labs, biotech startups, and pharma R&D.

RNA Secondary Structure Prediction
Minimum free energy folding, partition functions, and ensemble analysis using ViennaRNA, RNAfold, and custom pipeline development. Comparative and kinetic approaches included.
ViennaRNA · RNAfold · RNAalifold
Co-transcriptional Folding
Energy landscape analysis of RNA folding during transcription. Barriers algorithm, disconnectivity graphs, and kinetic pathway identification for riboswitches and regulatory elements.
Barriers · Treekin · Energy Landscapes
De Novo RNA Design
Computational design of functional RNA elements including xrRNAs, ribozymes, and PRF-inducing pseudoknots. Inverse folding, NUPACK, and structure-guided sequence optimization.
xrRNA · Pseudoknots · Inverse Folding
Machine Learning for RNA
Neural network approaches to RNA structure prediction, sequence-structure mapping, and fitness landscape modelling. Integration of ML models into wet-lab design pipelines.
Deep Learning · Structure Prediction · NLP
Comparative RNA Virology
Evolutionary analysis of viral RNA structures. Conservation-guided identification of functional elements in flavivirus, alphavirus, and other positive-sense RNA virus genomes.
Arboviruses · Structural Conservation · Phylogenetics
Grant & Manuscript Support
Computational strategy design for FWF, ERC, and NIH grant proposals. Manuscript co-authorship, peer review preparation, and figure production for RNA structure papers.
FWF · ERC · Collaboration
