RNA Structure Consultancy
-------------------------

MFE folding, partition functions, base pair probabilities, and ensemble
diversity analysis tailored to your RNA system. Co-transcriptional folding
pathway analysis using DrTransformer and BarMap. Energy landscape construction
to characterise metastable states and transition rates. 3D modelling with SimRNA and Amber. Custom pipeline
development with the ViennaRNA Python API. Available to academic labs,
biotech, and pharma R&D.

.. container:: tools-list

   **Tools:** RNAfold · RNAalifold · Barriers · DrTransformer

De Novo RNA Design
------------------

Computational design of functional RNA elements including xrRNA resistance
structures, PRF-inducing pseudoknots, riboswitches, and synthetic mRNA
constructs. Inverse folding with RNAinverse and advanced design with Infrared. Designs are
delivered with a predicted folding ensemble and a wet-lab validation
strategy, so experimental teams know exactly what to test.

.. container:: tools-list

   **Tools:** RNAinverse · Infrared · ViennaRNA

ViennaRNA Training
------------------

**Foundations workshop (4 h, online):** RNA secondary structure basics,
RNAfold, base pair probability analysis, and practical pipeline design.
Suited for experimentalists and graduate students entering computational
RNA biology.

**Advanced workshop (full day):** Energy landscapes, kinetic and co-transcriptional
folding, Barriers, Kinfold, and ViennaRNA Python API scripting. Designed
for computational biologists who want to go beyond single-structure
prediction.

Custom in-lab training is available on request and can be adapted to your
group's specific tools, workflows, and research questions.
